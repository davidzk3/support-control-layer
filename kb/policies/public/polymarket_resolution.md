Title: Market Resolution and Settlement

Source: Polymarket Documentation (public)

Type: public\_doc



Markets resolve based on predefined criteria and trusted sources. Resolution may involve third-party oracle systems.



A market may end in real-world time but remain open until the resolution process is finalized.



Users should not assume that event completion equals settlement. The platform may require additional time to process and reflect final outcomes.



Disputes may occur if resolution criteria are ambiguous or if source hierarchy is unclear.
